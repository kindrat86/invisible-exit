Frequently asked questions

Is the Non-Compete Scanner free?

Yes — completely free, no signup required.

Do you store or read my contract?

No. The entire scan runs in JavaScript inside your browser. Your contract text is never transmitted, uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere. Load the page, disconnect from the internet, and the scan still works.

What clauses does it detect?

Eight clause families: non-compete covenants, non-solicitation, invention/IP assignment, moonlighting and outside-employment bans, exclusivity ("full time and attention") clauses, confidentiality/trade-secret provisions, conflict-of-interest policies, and employer device/monitoring provisions.

Is this legal advice?

No — education only. Enforcement varies by state and by contract wording. Consult a licensed employment attorney in your jurisdiction before acting.
