# Product Hunt draft

Status: prepared only. Do not publish without Maryan's explicit approval.

## Product name

Invisible Exit

## Tagline

Build a side SaaS without building a personal brand

## Short description

Five tools for employed professionals who want to plan, validate, launch, and track a faceless micro-SaaS from one workspace.

## Maker comment

I built Invisible Exit around a constraint I kept seeing: many employed professionals are interested in a small software business, but they do not want a public founder identity attached to it.

The product combines five jobs in one workspace:

- define the financial target the project needs to serve;
- evaluate and narrow software ideas;
- review operational separation from an employer;
- plan a launch;
- keep a faceless brand consistent.

This is not a promise of income, anonymity, or legal clearance. Employment contracts and local rules differ, so the product provides planning tools rather than legal advice.

I would appreciate feedback on the first-run experience. Which tool would you expect to open first, and what would stop you from using it?
