## Title

Show HN: Invisible Exit, five tools for building a side SaaS without a personal brand

## Text

I built Invisible Exit for employed professionals who want to test a small software business without turning themselves into a public founder.

It brings five jobs into one workspace: working out what financial target the project needs to serve, evaluating ideas, checking operational separation from an employer, planning a launch, and keeping a faceless brand consistent.

The product is opinionated about one constraint: the side project should run on the founder's own time, equipment, and information. It does not replace legal advice, and it cannot tell someone whether a specific employment contract allows a project.

The current version is live at https://invisibleexit.com.

I would value blunt feedback on two things:

1. Is the faceless-founder constraint useful, or does it make the product too narrow?
2. Which of the five tools would you expect to use first?
