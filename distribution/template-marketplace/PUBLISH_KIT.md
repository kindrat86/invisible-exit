Before you build anything, answer one question: what does your employment contract actually say?

This 15-minute self-audit scores your risk across six sections:
1. Contract clauses — the 7 clauses to physically find in your agreement
2. IP-assignment deep-dive — the clause that catches most people (incl. California §2870-style carve-outs)
3. Overlap with your employer — the questions that decide if your idea is safe
