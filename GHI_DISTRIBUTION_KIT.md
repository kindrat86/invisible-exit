## The headline numbers (all real BLS data)

- Finance workers quit at **1.0%/month** — hospitality workers at **4.2%/month** (4x gap)
- Information sector = highest pay in America (**$107,456/yr avg**) + one of the lowest quits rates → GHI 79.8
- Financial Activities: **$96,151/yr**, 4.7-yr median tenure → GHI 78.9
- Government = GHI 100: quits rate 0.8%/mo, 6.2-yr median tenure
- US median tenure fell to **3.9 years in 2024 — lowest since 2002** — yet the quits rate ALSO fell to 2.0%. People aren't staying because they're happy; leaving got more expensive. ("Job lock")
- Sources: BLS JOLTS (Jun 2026), BLS Employee Tenure (Jan 2024), BLS CES (Jul 2026)
